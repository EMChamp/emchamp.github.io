winnow ↗
A four-stage Python pipeline that scores 700+ 4K clips on per-frame motion and sharpness, then cuts the dead footage. NVDEC hardware decode took the render from 1.03x to 3.90x realtime.
What brokeA "cut anything still for twelve seconds" rule would have carved 22 seconds out of a beautifully composed held wide shot. A parked camera and a deliberate one are identical to the metric. Intent is not measurable, so the heuristic had to go.
Agentic content pipeline
Local Ollama models driven by an open-source agent framework, generating JSON-constrained game content that Python validates before import into the engine.
What brokeUnconstrained generation does not survive contact with a schema. The real design work was drawing the line between what the model produces and what deterministic code enforces afterward.
Face-expression classifier
DeepFace and MediaPipe over 905 photos, identifying one specific child among family group shots and bucketing by expression.
What brokeThe off-the-shelf emotion model confidently read a serious closed-mouth face as distress. A mouth-aspect-ratio feature the model had no access to was what actually separated the classes. Model cards are not eval sets.
Household finance dashboard
Python PDF extraction across 1,434 bank and card transactions, reconciled against printed statement totals, feeding a dependency-free offline dashboard.
What brokeCharge-and-refund pairs quietly inflated spend by $8.6K until they were detected and netted out. Reconciling against the printed total is what surfaced it. Always check the extraction against a number the source already committed to.
